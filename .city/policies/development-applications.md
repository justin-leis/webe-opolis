# Development applications

**Adoption status:** The governing status of this exact revision is recorded in `.city/config.json`. Check `city status` and `city validate` before relying on it. Initialization alone does not adopt a revision or grant authority. The adoption record is a resident declaration and is not authenticated.

Effective only through resident adoption with the revised charter appointing the
Development Advisor. Installation, initialization and proposal preparation do not
appoint that office or adopt this policy. Existing cities must explicitly amend
their charter and policy register; existing authorizations are not revoked.

## Baseline materiality

The Chief Planner professionally screens each request against this plugin-wide
baseline and applicable adopted local refinements. Residents supply needs,
desired outcomes and observations, not engineered proposals. Ordinary new features
are not automatically formal applications. Routine documentation, tests and
contained repairs normally use the lightweight route unless material effects arise.
No formal application is not an exemption from authorization, inspection or final
resident acceptance.

Formal applications are required for new projects or substantial new capabilities;
material architecture or ownership-boundary changes; public-interface changes or
data migrations; significant security, operational, cost or cross-project effects.
Record explicit material impacts, reasons and evidence. A lightweight determination
cannot coexist with an explicit material impact.

Unknown jurisdiction, project scope, materiality thresholds or policy interpretation
requires focused resident questions and a `needs-clarification` determination, not
guessed scope, a presumed exemption or automatic heavyweight paperwork. The city
must investigate available technical facts itself. Ask residents only for intent,
choices or facts unavailable to the city.

## Scoped local refinements and authority

Record refinements in this policy or another named `.city/policies/*.md` policy,
with a section stating repository-wide applicability or exact registered project
IDs, thresholds, rationale, and the resident adoption decision. Adopt changes with
`amend`; cite the exact policy path, section/interpretation and project IDs in each
screening (`[]` means repository-wide). Project rules apply only to their named
projects. The Planner must account for every applicable refinement, not select only
convenient rules. Local rules may clarify or strengthen the baseline, never silently
waive it. Unresolved interpretation or conflicting rules go to the resident.
The CLI validates evidence structure and snapshots, not the semantic truth of prose
or completeness of a professional policy interpretation.

## Application assistance

A formal screening automatically assigns the named Development Advisor through the
City Manager, without a second resident request for assistance. The Advisor helps
define a proportionate proposal, relevant diagrams/drawings, specifications, project
facts and appropriate technical studies/evidence. The Planner lists only necessary
requirements with individual justifications; neither a master plan nor a full impact
matrix is compulsory for every request. Evidence may be provided or explained as
not applicable; non-applicability must justify why the underlying question is
adequately resolved, not conceal a missing necessary study. Missing required
evidence or resident answers blocks readiness. Unperformed studies remain missing;
static analysis is not an executed performance, security or compatibility study.

Request engineering or specialist investigation through City Manager within existing
authority, tool and resource limits. Advisory investigation authorizes no production
implementation, tool installation, paid studies, expenditure or permission increases.
The Advisor does not assess or approve its own application or grant authority.

## Independent assessment and persistence

Chief Planner independently assesses the exact current Advisor application revision
against the current screening and adopted scoped rules. Record `ready` or `revise`,
reasons and evidence. The Advisor must not substitute for this assessment. The
Council and resident retain delivery decisions, Inspector independent delivery
inspection, and resident final acceptance.

RSO, Advisor and Planner prepare records/CLI commands for the Records Officer
through City Manager; no terminal access is implied. Records Officer persists
`screen-development`, `application-record` and `assess-application` revisions on the
canonical work order. Office labels and evidence remain unauthenticated.
All revisions are preserved. Re-screening, changed project/proposal/policy context,
re-triage, reopening or return-to-proposed require current preparation before the
next authorization. Ordinary triaged-to-proposed progression does not invalidate
screening. Earlier authorized delivery is not retroactively invalidated. Reject,
return and refer remain available to resolve incomplete cases.
